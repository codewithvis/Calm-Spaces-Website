# C.A.L.M Spaces Brand System

## Brand architecture

**C.A.L.M Spaces** is the student-facing wellbeing identity.

**Centre of Excellence for Andragogical and Pedagogical Sciences (CEAPS)** provides the academic and institutional framework.

**SGT University** provides the institutional identity.

The relationship is expressed as:

`C.A.L.M Spaces · CEAPS · SGT University`

Do not merge the CALM Spaces and SGT University logos into a new logo.

## Official positioning

C.A.L.M Spaces is a holistic well-being unit for students at SGT University, functioning under the aegis of CEAPS. It supports students through counselling, peer support, support groups, listening circles, workshops, psycho-educational resources, reflective practices, and movement-based expressive activities.

## Official CALM phrase

Use: **Cultivating Awareness, Lightening Stress, and Healing through Movement.**

The letter A is not independently expanded in this system until the institution provides approved wording. Do not invent a fourth word to force an acronym layout.

## Visual direction

- Warm ivory and parchment neutrals establish a welcoming room.
- Deep forest and charcoal green provide institutional grounding.
- Sage supports wellbeing states without turning the interface entirely green.
- Clay and restrained lavender provide emotional and editorial accents.
- Fraunces provides editorial warmth; DM Sans provides readable UI structure.
- Photography must be real SGT campus, student, program, or approved team imagery.
- Official logos retain supplied proportions, colors, typography, and clear space.

## Voice

Warm, clear, student-friendly, respectful, and preventive. Use “Talk to someone,” “Find your space,” “Come find us,” and “Take a moment.” Avoid diagnosis, inflated claims, startup language, and unsupported clinical or privacy promises.

## Asset governance

Official CALM Spaces and SGT University logos are blocked until supplied or located at an authoritative official source. The current repository must not generate replacements.
