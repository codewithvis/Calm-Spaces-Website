# C.A.L.M Spaces Information Architecture

## Public navigation

Home · Find Support · Wellness · Resources · Events · Community · About · Team

Secondary: Counselling · C.A.L.M Companions · Stories · Impact · App · Visit Us · Help · Safety · Privacy · Contact

## Authenticated app navigation

Your Space: Home, Check-in, Journal, Patterns

Explore: Wellness, Create, Resources, Community, Saved

Support: Talk Space, Buddy Connect, Experts, Appointments

More: Notifications, Settings, Safety, Profile

## Signature journey

1. Student arrives at “You deserve a space to pause.”
2. Student selects what feels helpful now.
3. Calm Spaces maps the need to counselling, peer support, wellness, mood/journal, community, creative expression, or resources.
4. Student sees clear privacy and safety boundaries.
5. Student can explore publicly or sign in to save and continue.

## Public content model

- Institutional identity and verified bios
- Services and support pathways
- Events and registration
- Resources and editorial content
- Impact and approved stories
- Team and official profile links
- Campus location and visit information
- App connection
- Safety and privacy

## Content governance

Regularly changing content such as events, impact, stories, team records, FAQs, and resources should move to approved CMS/Supabase content tables only after their schema and RLS are verified. Static pages may contain only approved, source-traceable institutional copy.
