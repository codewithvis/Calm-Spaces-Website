- Add official location, patron, lead/coordinator, CEAPS context, and contact content.
- Add campus-aware public journeys: Find Support, Counselling, C.A.L.M Companions, Events, Visit Us, Team, Impact, Stories, and App.
- Replace invented/placeholder student and team identities with verified content or marked initials placeholders.

## First discriminating check

Search repository and Android reference assets for official CALM Spaces and SGT University logos. Current result: no approved logo assets are present in the web repository; the Android assets currently contain app artwork and utility assets, not an authenticated institutional logo set.

## Release risk

Do not launch the official institutional site with a generated logo, invented team photographs, unverified opening hours, unverified statistics, or unsupported claims of anonymity/confidentiality.
