# Calm Spaces Design Direction

## Brand idea

**A little space for you.** Calm Spaces is a quiet digital room: warm enough to invite someone in, structured enough to help them choose a next step, and clear enough to earn trust.

## Visual language

- Warm off-white and paper-like surfaces instead of pure white everywhere.
- Soft sage as the primary action and recovery color.
- Warm sand for invitation and grounding.
- Muted lavender-blue for reflective or informational accents.
- Deep green-charcoal for readable text and nighttime calm.
- Restrained borders and low, broad shadows. Avoid card piles and decorative blobs.
- Editorial typography for emotional headings, highly readable sans-serif for controls and body copy.

## Semantic color roles

- **Sage:** primary actions, support, positive progress.
- **Sand:** welcome surfaces, selected discovery paths, gentle emphasis.
- **Blue-lavender:** reflection, resources, informational states.
- **Terracotta:** warmth and occasional creative accents.
- **Amber:** caution and non-urgent attention.
- **Rose:** errors and urgent safety states only.
- **Deep green-charcoal:** text, navigation, and high-contrast content.

## Layout

- Public pages use wide editorial bands and unframed content sections.
- The authenticated home uses a calm two-column composition on desktop and a linear, breathable flow on mobile.
- Repeated items may use restrained cards; pages themselves should not become nested card grids.
- Touch targets remain at least 44px and content must work at 320px wide.

## Motion

- Fast: 120ms for hover and focus feedback.
- Normal: 240ms for drawers, tabs, and navigation transitions.
- Slow: 480ms for wellness immersion only.
- Every motion rule must have a reduced-motion fallback.

## Voice

Use “Take a moment,” “Find support,” “Write something,” “Try this,” “Not right now,” and “Continue.” Avoid “Submit,” “Execute,” “Optimize,” and guilt-based reminders.

## Accessibility baseline

- WCAG 2.2 AA contrast targets.
- Semantic headings and landmarks.
- Visible keyboard focus.
- Reduced motion support.
- Accessible chart summaries.
- No meaning conveyed by color alone.
- Clear privacy explanations near sensitive features.
